# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in
Groundtrail, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence in good faith.
- Respect differing technical conclusions and ask clarifying questions.
- Give actionable feedback about work rather than personal criticism.
- Protect confidential information and report security concerns privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or sharing private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a concern through the private security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers
will review reports promptly, preserve confidentiality where practical, and take proportionate action.

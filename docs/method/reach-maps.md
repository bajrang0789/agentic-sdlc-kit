# Reach Maps

A Reach Map records impact without turning inference into fact. Relationships are `direct`, `shared-code`, `runtime-upstream`, `runtime-downstream`, `data`, `security`, and `unknown`. Each entry states whether its derivation is `observed` or `inferred` and retains source references.

Entries may include criticality, security and data flags, owner and runbook references, confidence, and unresolved unknowns. A Reach Map is revision-bound: an impacted dependency or implementation change may require a new map or an explicit reevaluation.
